Who can give tasks to whom

  • Everyone can create tasks for themselves.
  • Team Leads and Department Heads can give tasks to the people in their own department who rank below them, and to anyone who reports to them. They cannot give tasks to peers, to their own boss, or to other departments.
  • Admins can give tasks to anyone.
  • Interns and Employees cannot give tasks to others. To ask another department for work, send a department request instead.

Each employee's "reports to" line is set when they are added, and can be changed on their profile.

Roles, employment types and exceptions

  • A role (Intern, Employee, Team Lead, Department Head, Admin, or any role your administrator creates) decides what a person can do.
  • An employment type ({% for k, n in etypes %}{{ n }}{{ ', ' if not loop.last }}{% endfor %}) describes the contract. It is a label for HR and reports; it does not change rights by itself.
  • A manager can grant or remove one specific right for one person under Employees > person > Rights, without changing their whole role. You can only hand out rights you hold yourself.
  • Some areas are also limited by department (for example Finance, Recruitment, CSR). Those limits apply on top of the roles above.
